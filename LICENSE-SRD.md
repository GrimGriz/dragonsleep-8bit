## What DRAGONSLEEP takes from the SRD 5.1

- **Rules:** ability modifiers, proficiency bonus, attack rolls against AC, saving throws against DCs, advantage and disadvantage, critical hits, the conditions it uses (poisoned, frightened, restrained, prone, paralyzed, grappled, blinded, stunned, unconscious), short and long rests, experience points by challenge rating, the level table to 5.
- **Classes (levels 1–5):** fighter (Champion), rogue, wizard (School of Evocation), paladin (Oath of Devotion) — hit dice, spell slots, Second Wind, Action Surge, Sneak Attack, Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge, Arcane Recovery, Lay on Hands, Divine Smite, Fighting Styles, Channel Divinity (Sacred Weapon), Extra Attack. Half-orc traits (Relentless Endurance, Savage Attacks).
- **Spells:** fire bolt, light, dancing lights, burning hands, magic missile, shield, sleep, detect magic, find familiar, mage armor, thunderwave, scorching ray, shatter, web, hold person, fireball, lightning bolt, bless, cure wounds, shield of faith, divine favor, heroism, lesser restoration, aid.
- **Creatures:** giant rat, swarm of rats, darkmantle, gray ooze, ochre jelly, otyugh, giant bat, swarm of bats, stirge, cloaker, giant spider, giant wolf spider, ettercap, gnoll, hyena, will-o'-wisp, giant fire beetle, giant frog, poisonous snake, swarm of insects, bandit, thug, bandit captain, guard, veteran, wolf, worg, axe beak, giant boar, giant centipede, berserker, chuul.
- **Equipment and magic items:** the simple and martial weapons, armor and shields, potions of healing, healer's kit, antitoxin, oil, torches, rope, tent, +1 weapons, ring of protection.

Game terms are simplified for a turn-based menu game ("5E-flavored, FF-simple"). Three creatures in the game are **not** from the SRD and are this game's own stat blocks: the Crawler, the Keeper, and the Glory-Seeker (see `invented.json`).

Nothing else in this repository is SRD material. DRAGONSLEEP, Silverton, the corridor, and every person, place and name in the game belong to GrimGriz.
