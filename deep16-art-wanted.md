# DEEP16 art wanted

Nothing here blocks a fight: every one of these plays today on a stand-in. These are looks, not needs. A free print model with a .blend can stand in for any creature sheet below (pipeline 1b, `deep16/blender-monsters.md`).

## 1. Creatures still on a stand-in (one block a creature, every sheet it needs)

Worst fit first. Since 10-08 a block is a whole creature (Griz: *"Do the 'attached' paragraph (or the description paragraph) and then all the 'HEAD' entries and a single tail at the bottom"*): the look once, a HEAD for each sheet -- each comes back as its own image -- and one tail. **Every block was re-cut to ISOMETRIC on 10-08 evening, on his words:** *"it has just occurred to me the art department is putting out sprites thinking this is a 2D game. It's now generating based on isometry."* and *"art wanted .md apparently needs amending"*. The game is an isometric grid with eight facings, and a step along a grid axis faces a screen diagonal; the old convention here (every frame in side view facing right, plus a sheet from the front and one from behind) came from the first cutter (Denny's, 09-27: *"the side views take the diagonals"*) and was frozen into this file, and it made everything walk side-on on an isometric floor. GreyFang's four isometric sheets (cut 10-08, `tools/greyfang-sheet.py`) proved the new way. Asked *"two isometric views per creature (bottom-right and top-right, mirrored for the other two), or four?"* he answered *"1 mirrored"*. So a block is two HEADs: HEAD 1 is the creature from the bottom-right, every frame facing toward the bottom-right corner of the image, with the portrait and the turnaround; HEAD 2 is the same moves from the top-right, seen from behind, every frame facing toward the top-right corner, rows only; the cutter mirrors both for the other two facings. A row asked from the side goes on both sheets; a row asked only from the front stays on HEAD 1; a climb is asked from behind, so on HEAD 2 only (a wall is climbed facing it); Fall, Prone, a still and anything that reads the same from every side go on both. A thing with no face and no back -- the oozes, the mouther, the mound, the fungus -- gets HEAD 1 only, every row, plus a HEAD 2 that carries only its climb when it has one. Every row keeps one frame count in every facing. The line under each block is for the seat that cuts it.
