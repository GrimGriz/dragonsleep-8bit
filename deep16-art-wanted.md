# DEEP16 art wanted

The order is the story fights first (the ones that now run on the grid from the 8-bit game), worst stand-in first: a creature drawn today as some other model (the cloaker is a ghost, the ettercap an alien, the otyugh a green blob, the roper a cactus). Two a day: day 1 is the first pair, and so on. Canon notes are from the wiki and the monster records; the look past them is SRD-plain.

What the cutter needs from a sheet (the gnoll sheets were right): a turnaround of stills (front, right, back, left), then rows of frames all facing right in side view; every frame apart from its neighbours; one scale for the whole sheet. Colour needn't be exact: every sheet is regraded and snapped to DEEP16's 64 colours. A second sheet for the same creature (idle variations, special moves) is welcome, never needed.

---

## COMMON TAIL (paste after every head)

16-bit pixel art animation sheet, SNES-era action RPG style, crisp dark outline, readable at small size. Flat dark navy background. Top left: one large portrait. Beside it a turnaround of four stills labelled Front, Right, Back, Left. Below: labelled rows of frames, every frame in side view facing right, numbered under each frame. Every frame stands apart with clear space around it: nothing overlaps a neighbour or a label. The same scale in every row, feet on one baseline. No scenery except what a row names.

## Day 1

### 1. The cloaker (the deep gallery; the gimmick's close-up)

Stands in as a ghost today, and the gimmick's storyboard ends on "a big close-up of the cloaker's face".

HEAD: CLOAKER (cave manta) -- Deep Gallery. A cave predator that looks like a great black leather cloak: a manta-ray body with wide wings, ivory hook-claws along the wing edges, a long whip tail, the underside pale with a fanged mouth and two glowing red eyes. It glides above the floor. Rows: Idle (hovering, wings rippling, 8), Glide (8), Attack (wraps forward and bites, 8), Moan (wings flared, the air darkening around it, 6), Hurt (6), Death (collapses to a heap of cloak, 8). Idle variation: its wing-hooks braiding a three-strand cord. Also, separately, one large close-up of its face: the red eyes and the fanged mouth, filling a square panel.

### 2. The ettercap (Web Gulch, the first story fight moved to the grid)

Stands in as an alien today.

HEAD: ETTERCAP (web-keeper) -- Web Gulch. A hunched, spindly humanoid with a spider's face: mandibles, a cluster of small black eyes, coarse bristles. Long many-jointed arms ending in hooked claws, a swollen belly and back, purple-grey chitinous skin, spinnerets under its jaw. Rows: Idle (8), Walk (hunched, 8), Attack (claws, 8), Bite (6), Web (throws a line of silk to the right, 6), Hurt (6), Death (8). Idle variation: sitting, braiding a three-strand cord of silk with its claws.
