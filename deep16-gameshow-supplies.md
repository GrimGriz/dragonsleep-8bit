## The seat's opinion on the items (yours to overrule)

- **Tier 2 is the rich one.** +2 AC and a +1 weapon at 1st-3rd level is more than most heroes have. It may outshine tier 3's +1. The bench will say; the easy lever is tier 2's armour at 15 (+1), not 16.
- **Leans:** 2g (his bolt is his blow), 20a, 22b (already built), 23a (no new code), 24a (the name fits a sponsor), 25a (most foes hit with ordinary weapons, so it halves most damage).
- **Attunement on** means the ring, the cloak and the amulet are exactly three. A tier 4 item that needs attuning (23b, 23c, 24b) is the fourth, so someone takes something off. That's the choice you wanted.
- **On shoves:**
  - **Dwarves:** they have nothing against being shoved, in the SRD or in our game. In our game they resist poison. Holding ground against shoves was a 3.5-era dwarf trait (Stability; that's from memory, not checked).
  - **Denny:** he's the one who shoves. From 3rd, Denim Damage knocks foes back with no save, and Cannonball knocks them prone. Stand Firm at 3rd already gives him advantage against being shoved or knocked down.
  - **24c:** the runner's "suits Denny" note was backwards. It fits Goose or Rascal, if anyone.
