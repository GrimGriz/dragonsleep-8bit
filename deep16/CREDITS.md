# DEEP16 — art credits

Every sprite sheet in `deep16/art/` is made by the tools in `tools/` from the sources below. The engine's floor, walls,
stalagmites, cocoons and rubble are drawn in code (`deep16/js/iso.js`, `deep16/js/art.js`); the font is the 8-bit game's own.

## Pipeline 2 — generated (`denny_p2`, `chuul_p2`, `crawler_p2`, `gnoll_p2`, `gloryseeker_p2`, `cloaker_p2`, `ettercap_p2`, `otyugh_p2`, `hyena_p2`, `bulette_p2`, `owl_p2`, `snowyowl_p2`)

- **Denny** — a character sheet generated by Griz (GrimGriz), 2026-09-27, cleaned to the palette by `tools/denny-sheet.py`.
- **The chuul** — two sheets (a turnaround; the walk, the claw, the death) generated by Griz with Grok, 2026-09-27, regraded to wet stone and cleaned to the palette by `tools/chuul-sheet.py`. It replaces the Quaternius frog that stood in (`chuul_p1`, kept).
- **The crawler** — one sheet (three views; idle, crawl, tentacles, bite, hurt, death seen from the side) generated by Griz with Grok, 2026-09-27, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/crawler-sheet.py`, its colours as drawn; the top view stands for the north facing (there is no rear view). It replaces the snake stand-in (`crawler_p1`).
- **The gnolls** — two sheets (a turnaround; idle, walk, run, the spear, hurt, death from the side; idle variations, back views and more) generated by Griz, 2026-09-28, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/gnoll-sheet.py`: the gnoll in the Hills tribe's blue as drawn (`gnoll_p2`), the Snoot's glory-seekers recoloured to the Desert tribe's red (`gloryseeker_p2`). They replace the Quaternius Tribal stand-ins (`gnoll_p1`, `gloryseeker_p1`, kept).

- **The cloaker** (`cloaker_p2`) — two sheets (the roost and the side flight; the manta from the front and the back) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, keyed off their checkerboard, posed into the hover, glide, lunge and death, and snapped to the palette by `tools/cloaker-sheet.py`. It replaces the Flying Ghost stand-in (`cloaker_p1`).
- **The ettercap** (`ettercap_p2`) — two sheets (movement and core: idle, the walk four ways, hit, death; combat and specials: bite, claws, the web shot, recovery and more) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/ettercap-sheet.py`, the hide's purple lifted so it survives the snap. It replaces the Ultimate Monsters Alien stand-in (`ettercap_p1`, kept).
- **The otyugh** (`otyugh_p2`) — one sheet (a turnaround; half sunk in its pool, the rise, walk, tentacle slam, bite, hurt, death) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/otyugh-sheet.py`. It replaces the GreenSpikyBlob stand-in (`otyugh_p1`, kept).
- **The hyena** (`hyena_p2`) — one sheet (a turnaround; idle, walk, run, bite, laugh, hurt, death) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/hyena-sheet.py`. It replaces the Husky stand-in (`hyena_p1`, kept).
- **The bulette** (`bulette_p2`) — two sheets (idle, walk, burrow, emerge and the leap on a baked checkerboard; a turnaround, hurt, death, walks toward and away) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, keyed and snapped to the palette by `tools/bulette-sheet.py`. It replaces the Ultimate Monsters Dino stand-in (`bulette_p1`, kept).
- **The owls** (`owl_p2`, `snowyowl_p2`: Find Familiar's) — two sheets each (perch or stand, hover, fly, harass, flyby, hurt, vanish; turnarounds, flights toward and away) generated by Griz, 2026-09-29, cut and snapped to the palette by `tools/owl-sheet.py`.
