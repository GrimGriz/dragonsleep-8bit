The game's dialog scenes, played without loading a game: each is staged by the game's own show beat or door, the conditions on the left yours to change, and every line is the line the game would say, marked at its back with what chose it -- its series and depth, the flags read, the text key, who speaks. A note on any line is stamped with the scene, the line and the conditions; NOTES saves hero_situations_feedback-<UTC>.json as the M menu's NOTES does. Nothing here is saved to a game.
