# The 8-bit's reaction window and concentration -- notes for the overseer (10-03)

Branch `claude/8bit-reactions` (the cloud seat's; also on its session branch `claude/new-session-j8nd9v`), from `main` at c7f8451 plus the cherry-picked ae9af63 (the `DEEP16_BROWSER` knob). Ruled by Griz 10-03: *"Simple because of movement, but they should still get their reactions is my first impression"*; *"1 yes, 2 yes"*; the slot retired because *"it was limiting the 8bit characters to only carrying one buff"*; the slot stays lowest-first (*"3 yes"*).

## 1. What was built

**The reaction window** (`js/battle.js`, the block after `wardCheck`, before `// hero turn`):

- `u.reaction` on every unit (hero, guest, foe): 1 at the start of its turn (`Battle.turn`), 0 once spent. `canReact`: not down, not helpless. `sees(u, w)`: not blinded, not blind in the dark, the invisible unseen unless See Invisibility, the snake's sense or the Mirror's eye.
- Two triggers, no squares. **A hit about to land** (the d20 known, the damage not): `foeAttack` asks `shieldReact` before the miss check (+5 AC till the hero's next turn; `conds.shielded`, which `turn()` already cleared), and `dodgeReact` after the damage roll (Uncanny Dodge, rogue 5+, the attacker seen). A hero's blow at a foe asks `foeParry` (a sheet's `reactions: { parry: 2 }` -- the Bandit Captain and Hask have it now in `content/monsters.json`: +2 AC against one melee hit that would land by less than 2, the attacker seen; the spiritual weapon's swing too). **A creature you can see casts a spell**: `special()` asks `counterAsk` first when the special carries `spell: "<record id>"`; countered, the special is spent (its recharge or once) and nothing happens. **Hellish Rebuke** rides on the hurt: `rebuke(t, f)` after a foe's blow (after the blow's own rider) and after a blast special that dealt damage. No opportunity attacks.
- The player's hero is asked with a `DS.choose` menu that has a way to decline (`askReact`; B = let it go): SHIELD? (CAST SHIELD / LET IT LAND), HELLISH REBUKE? (REBUKE <foe> / LET IT GO), COUNTERSPELL? (COUNTER (NO CHECK) or COUNTER (CHECK DC n) / LET IT GO), UNCANNY DODGE? (DODGE IT, "take n" / TAKE IT). A guest or AI-run hero decides as the grid's AI does: Shield whenever it turns the hit, Hellish Rebuke always, Counterspell at any levelled spell, Uncanny Dodge at 6 damage or more. Shield is offered only when it turns the blow (a hit by less than 5, never a natural 20): the grid's rule.
- The reaction spells are off the MAGIC list (`heroTurn` filters `sp.reaction`); `reactSpell(u, id)` says from which slot one would go (lowest that holds it; 0 when not known, not prepared or no slot). Records: `shield` gets `"reaction": "hit"`, `hellishrebuke` `"battle": true, "reaction": "hurt"`, `counterspell` `"battle": true, "reaction": "cast"`. Hellish Rebuke: (1 + slot)d10 fire, the foe's DEX save for half, the caster's DC. Counterspell: automatic at the slot's level or below, else d20 + the casting ability against 10 + the spell's level. Magic Missile does nothing to one shielded (the blast branch of `special()`, for a special tagged `magicmissile`).
- Uncanny Dodge was automatic in the 8-bit (the first hit a round halved, `conds.dodged`): now the reaction it is, asked, spent. A rogue who knows Shield picks one.

**Concentration** (the same block; `u.conc = { id, name, rounds, marks, cloud?, litWas? }`):

- `"conc": true` on **82 records** in `content/spells.json`, by the grid's `deep16/data/durations.js` (`c: 1`, the SRD's text) plus Mirror's Gaze (game-original, concentration by its own words). **19 of the 50 battle records**: Bless, Divine Favor, Fog Cloud, Heroism, Hideous Laughter, Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith, Dancing Lights, Web, Branding Smite, Hold Person, Invisibility, Magic Weapon, Sleet Storm, Stinking Cloud, Greater Invisibility, Stoneskin, Hold Monster, Mislead.
- `castSpell` calls `beginConc` for a `conc` record (a caster holding another spell lets it go with a card: "Lymen lets go of Shield of Faith (a new spell)." then "Barley's shield of faith fades."; the same spell cast again ends the first the same way) and `concMark` after the targets loop: every condition this cast laid (`CONC_KEYS`: bless → blessed, holdperson → paralyzed, web → restrained, invisibility → invisible, and so on) is marked `by` the caster's id. A cloud carries `by`; Dancing Lights remembers whether the place was lit before.
- A blow on the caster (`hurt`, the hero branch) calls `concCheck`: CON save, DC 10 or half the damage, the higher; the line rides `pendingMsg` after the hit's own ("Lymen holds Divine Favor. (CON 22 vs DC 10)" / "Lymen loses Divine Favor! (CON 3 vs DC 10) Lymen's divine favor fades."). Down lets go (the ko branch of `hurt`); helpless lets go (`concSweep` at the top of every turn, and right after a rider or a curse that paralyses or stuns the caster). `dropConc` takes back only the conditions still marked `by` this caster (a later cast of the same condition by another hand took the mark over), clears the caster's cloud, puts the dancing lights out (the dark comes back unless a torch, a burning blade, a Continual Flame or the Light cantrip -- `litSpell` -- stands). `endTurn`: a `conc` with nothing live left is dropped quietly; the minute's end takes the rest ("has run its minute").
- Ingrith's own Bless (`clericTurn`) concentrates too, and she will not Bless while holding another spell.
- Only heroes and guests concentrate: the 8-bit's foes cast their sheets' specials, not records (unchanged).

**The buff slot retired**: `u.buff` is gone from every unit (`Battle`, `joinParty`, `makeFoe`, `R.makeHero`, `R.refresh`). Bless → `conds.blessed` (the d4 in `heroAttack` and `save`), Shield of Faith → `conds.shieldOfFaith` (+2 in `acOf`), Heroism → `conds.heroism` (the temp HP in `hurt` and at the turn's start, the fear ward in the moan), Divine Favor → `conds.divineFavor` (+1d4 radiant). Each `{ rounds: 10 }`, several at once on one hero, ended by its caster's concentration or its own ten rounds (`END_TEXT` has their lines). Sacred Weapon is Channel Divinity, not concentration: `conds.sacred` for the minute, beside any blessing. `R.CONDS` tags them on the panel (BLS, SOF, HRO, DVF, SWD, SHD), and a caster's held spell shows as `*BLE`-style. `js/spellfx8.js` reads the conditions where it read the slot.

**The Counterspell targets** (`content/monsters.json`, seven line-targeted edits, no re-dump): `spell` tags on the spell-weaver's bolt storm (lightningbolt), hold (holdperson) and darkness; Torvald's guardians (spiritguardians), hold and sanctuary; the naga's bolt and hold; the drow's and the captain's darkness. The darkmantle's darkness is its aura and stays untagged; Amara's Eldritch Blast is an attack on her sheet, not a special.

**The gate**: `dev/bench8.py` has the `DEEP16_BROWSER` / `DEEP16_BROWSER_ARGS` lines (the same as bench16's); the new mode is in `ALL_SCRIPTS` in `dev/check.py`. Two lines in `invented.json` (`8bit-reaction-window-calls`, `8bit-specials-that-are-spells`). The register: seven rows in `spells-srd-by-class.md` say BUILT in the 8-bit 10-03 (Sacred Weapon has no row there: it is Channel Divinity, a class feature); `spells-two-books.md` §2a.1, §2a.2, §2a.7, §2c.4, seven table rows and §5 questions 1, 2 and 7 carry the ruling and the build.

## 3. Where this crossed the `claude/spell-fixes` lines

Named so the merge is looked at, not guessed:

- `hurt()`, the hero branch: the heroism soak line (was `u.buff.temp`), the ko line (adds `dropConc`), and a new line after the damage (`concCheck`). The foe branch is untouched.

The dice line (`DS.roll(sp.dmg)` once per target) was not touched -- the area roll is theirs.

- `foeAttack`: the lines before the miss check (Shield), the Uncanny Dodge line, and the end (the rider, then concentration, then the rebuke).

## 4. What was not seen

- **No pane.** The reaction menus were never looked at on the screen: `askReact` draws a `DS.choose` at x 24, w 208, with a title (the longest, "COUNTERSPELL? Protection from Evil and Good", would be wider than the box); the bench only called `draw()` once with the conditions up. The desktop's eye is wanted on one Shield ask and one Counterspell ask.
- **No fight driven through the menus with a reaction in it.** The new mode steps `foeAttack`, `special`, `castSpell` and `turn` by hand; the seven driven modes have no caster who would be asked (Aurdin's default day at 5th does not prepare Shield -- see question 3 -- and no one knows the other two). A real battle where the player answers SHIELD? and the turn goes on was not run.
- **Not benched:** a guest's automatic Shield (no guest knows it); the decline path (LET IT LAND and the rest return false: read, not run); Dancing Lights going out and the dark coming back; a cloud ending with its caster's concentration; Hold Person or Web ending when the caster is hit (the mark-and-drop is the same code as Bless's, which was benched); the Bandit Captain's Parry (Hask's was, from the same field); the Counterspell "begins to cast" card in a real spell-weaver fight; the roost.
- **Older saves:** nothing to migrate -- `buff` lived on the battle unit, never on the saved hero.
- `R.prepDefault` was not changed: a wizard who wants Shield as a reaction must have it prepared (the day's menu), as the SRD has it.
