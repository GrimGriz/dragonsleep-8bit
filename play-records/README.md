# Keeper playtest records

Griz's play records of the Keeper of the Flooded Stair (DEEP16, `?keeperfight&play=keeper`), as saved by the combat log: `keeper-seed<seed>-L<level>.txt`. Records saved before the log's `settings:` header (10-03) carry no settings line. In these files `result won` means the party won and `result lost` means the Keeper won.
