# The level choices, for DRAGONSLEEP

## 0. His rule (RULED 10-29)

- *"I want the players to be making the same decisions at level up as they would be playing in a version using the PHB and DMG, and where we have to we will event the options they're choosing between"* -- so each decision is counted as PHB-and-DMG play counts it, and where the SRD has fewer, ours are invented to make up the count.
- *"when applicable, our sufficiently distinct will be acronyms SD"* -- our options are named with the initials S. D. where a name will carry them (Stab Debut, his).
- *"SRD we haven't built yet may address my chief concerns"* (on the whole lists, §1) -- no inventions where the SRD's list is the PHB's; there, the work is building the SRD's own.
- *"SRD compliance failures in this area have been a large oversight and are of increased priority re: usage"* (on the first batch and the choice at 3 on the pick).
- *"I'd lean to let barbarians have it as well, but this is a paladin feat question"* (Symmetric Fighting) -- a lean; §4 question 1.

Each decision gets one verdict:

- **SHORT n**: PHB-and-DMG play offers more than the SRD; n of ours are wanted to make up the count. Drafted here for his word.
- **WHOLE**: the SRD's list is the PHB's list (the 2014 rules): nothing to invent; the work is building the SRD's own.
- **OURS**: our own, built and in the game.
- **DRAFT**: our own, drafted here; nothing built till his word.

**Sufficiently distinct, the test each draft carries:** its **NOT** line names the nearest PHB option(s) and what ours does not share -- not the trigger, not the effect, not the signature thing (superiority dice, another class's spells, a reroll, advantage for striking first). A name is never borrowed. Where a later non-SRD book has the nearest neighbor, it is named too, so the space is seen.

**The playable levels:** the first game caps at 9 (`R.CAP`); the second game's ladders and the Pocket DM go to 12. The drafts are written to 9; a subclass's 10th-and-later features wait in its row.

## 2. The first batch: what the four meet first

### 2.1 The fighter at 3: the Sod Dancer (DRAFT)

Barley's road: *"He went down once against Cutter on fight night and got back up"* (`content/heroes.json`), a Dominion farm wanderer with a threshing flail. A fighter who fights from the ground, gets up off it, and beats down what is on it.

- **3, Sod Dancing.** Prone is no trouble to the fighter: its melee attacks while prone have no disadvantage, a creature within 5 ft has no advantage against it for its being prone, and standing up costs 5 ft of movement.
- **3, Second Down.** Once per long rest, when the fighter is dropped to 0 HP and not killed, it rises at the start of its next turn with HP equal to its fighter level + its CON modifier, standing at no cost, and its first attack that turn has advantage.
- **7, Stamp Down.** Once on each of its turns, when the fighter hits a prone creature with a melee weapon attack, the hit deals one more weapon die.
- **10, 15, 18:** waiting, past the cap.

**NOT** the Battle Master (no superiority dice, no maneuvers -- it knocks nothing down, it fights what is down), nor the Eldritch Knight (no spells). Not the SRD's own Relentless Endurance (the half-orc's) or Relentless Rage (the barbarian's): those keep a creature from falling, and this one falls, loses its time, then gets back up. **The engines:** prone is on the grid (its prone rows, the bulette's knockdown, the Sand's, Grappler's PIN); a hero at 0 is down (KO) with no death saves in either battle, so the rise is at the start of its next turn.

### 2.2 The paladin at 3: the Oath of Soil's Debt (DRAFT)

Lymen's road: *"Self-ordained under Kalindel, the generative nature of the planet, in a town outside Newland's writ"* (`content/heroes.json`). An oath that gives back what the field takes.

- **Tenets.** What falls, feeds. Leave the ground richer than you found it. No salted earth.
- **Oath spells** (all SRD, none on Devotion's or the Ancients' lists): 3rd, Goodberry and Longstrider; 5th, Barkskin and Spike Growth; 9th, Daylight and Meld into Stone. Built on the grid: Longstrider, Barkskin, Spike Growth, Daylight; not built: Goodberry, Meld into Stone (a duration only).
- **3, Channel Divinity: Shared Debt.** An action: one creature at 0 HP within 30 ft that the paladin can see regains HP equal to the paladin's level and stands, and the paladin loses as many HP, which nothing reduces.
- **3, Channel Divinity: Sown Dead.** A reaction, when a creature the paladin can see drops to 0 HP within 30 ft: each friend within 10 ft of the one who fell regains 1d6 + the paladin's CHA modifier.
- **7, Aura: Sunk Deep.** The paladin and friends within 10 ft cannot be moved against their will or knocked prone (each may choose to be). At 18, 30 ft.
- **15, 20:** waiting.

**NOT** the Oath of the Ancients, the PHB's green oath, though Kalindel is the nature of the planet: its Nature's Wrath restrains with vines, its Turn the Faithless turns fey and fiends, its aura gives resistance to spell damage -- this one restrains nothing, turns nothing and wards against no spell. Not Vengeance (no vow of advantage, no fear), not the DMG's Oathbreaker (no undead, no aura of hate). Not the SRD's Devotion (no blessed weapon, no turning, no charm-proof aura). The nearest outside the PHB is the Crown's Turn the Tide (a bonus action that heals everyone hurt nearby); Sown Dead heals only those beside a foe that has just fallen. **The engines:** being pushed and knocked prone are on the grid (shove, the Sand's knockdown, Repelling Blast, Thunderwave, the roper's Reel).

### 2.4 Ours, built, read against the PHB

- **Cutthroat** (the rogue at 3, Vivian's other road): **BUILT 10-29, Stab Debut** in place of Opening Cut, on his word (*"replace 'opening cut' with 'Stab Debut' where she gets a straight weapon damage attack (no bonuses) on targets that haven't first-turned"*): with the Attack action at a foe that has not had its first turn, one more attack at it, the weapon's dice alone -- no ability modifier, no Sneak Attack, no rider; no advantage and no critical from it. The seat's reading: free, once a turn, the same foe while it stands. Both battles (`js/battle.js` heroAttack, `deep16/js/battle.js` stabDebut); `mode=stabdebut1029`, `dev/bench8.py stabdebut1029`. Opening Cut's trigger and both effects had been the PHB Assassin's Assassinate. **What it cut** (his question, the expected first blow against AC 13-15, Sneak Attack landing both ways): about 18 to 9.5 at 3rd level with the candle-knife, 27 to 14.5 at 5th, 35.5 to 19 at 8th -- about half, most of it the critical's doubled Sneak Attack; the stab itself is worth about 2.
- **The Path of the Sand's Answer Back** (the barbarian at 6): *one who misses in melee gets a blow back* has the trigger and effect of the PHB Battle Master's Riposte (a maneuver: a reaction attack on a melee miss), without the die and on another class. Still his look: his answer of 10-29 to question 5 named Cutthroat alone.
- **Read clean:** the Window, the Vigil, the Keeping, the Grey Road (Turn It Aside's nearest is the SRD's Protection style, with another effect), the Mirror, the Rimeglass, the Sand's First Blood and Down in the Sand.
