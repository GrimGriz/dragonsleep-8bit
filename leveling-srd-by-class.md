# The level choices, for DRAGONSLEEP

Each choice gets one verdict:

- **THIN**: the SRD gives one option (a subclass, the one feat). Ours are drafted here.
- **WHOLE**: the SRD's list is the PHB's list (the 2014 rules): nothing of the PHB's to replace. Breadth there is building more of the SRD's own.
- **OURS**: our own, built and in the game.
- **DRAFT**: our own, drafted here; nothing built till his word.
- **CLOSE**: one of ours, built, that sits near a PHB option -- for his look; nothing built is cut.

**Sufficiently distinct, the test each draft carries:** its **NOT** line names the nearest PHB option(s) and what ours does not share -- not the trigger, not the effect, not the signature thing (superiority dice, another class's spells, a reroll, advantage for striking first). A name is never borrowed. Where a later non-SRD book has the nearest neighbor, it is named too, so the space is seen.

**The playable levels** (CLAUDE.md's pull rule: the playable classes at the playable levels): the first game caps at 9 (`R.CAP`); the second game's ladders and the Pocket DM go to 12. The drafts are written to 9; a subclass's 10th-and-later features wait in its row.

### 2.1 The fighter at 3: the Threshing Floor (DRAFT)

Barley's road: *"He went down once against Cutter on fight night and got back up"* (`content/heroes.json`), a Dominion farm wanderer with a threshing flail. A fighter who fights from the ground, gets up off it, and beats down what is on it.

- **3, Hard Ground.** Prone is no trouble to the fighter: its melee attacks while prone have no disadvantage, a creature within 5 ft has no advantage against it for its being prone, and standing up costs 5 ft of movement.
- **3, Up Off the Floor.** Once per long rest, when the fighter is dropped to 0 HP and not killed, it rises at the start of its next turn with HP equal to its fighter level + its CON modifier, standing at no cost, and its first attack that turn has advantage.
- **7, Threshing.** Once on each of its turns, when the fighter hits a prone creature with a melee weapon attack, the hit deals one more weapon die.
- **10, 15, 18:** waiting, past the cap.

**NOT** the Battle Master (no superiority dice, no maneuvers -- it knocks nothing down, it fights what is down), nor the Eldritch Knight (no spells). Not the SRD's own Relentless Endurance (the half-orc's) or Relentless Rage (the barbarian's): those keep a creature from falling, and this one falls, loses its time, then gets back up. **The engines:** prone is on the grid (its prone rows, the bulette's knockdown, the Sand's, Grappler's PIN); a hero at 0 is down (KO) with no death saves in either battle, so the rise is at the start of its next turn.

### 2.2 The paladin at 3: the Oath of the Green Return (DRAFT)

Lymen's road: *"Self-ordained under Kalindel, the generative nature of the planet, in a town outside Newland's writ"* (`content/heroes.json`). An oath that gives back what the field takes.

- **Tenets.** What falls, feeds. Leave the ground richer than you found it. No salted earth.
- **Oath spells** (all SRD, none on Devotion's or the Ancients' lists): 3rd, Goodberry and Longstrider; 5th, Barkskin and Spike Growth; 9th, Daylight and Meld into Stone. Built on the grid: Longstrider, Barkskin, Spike Growth, Daylight; not built: Goodberry, Meld into Stone (a duration only).
- **3, Channel Divinity: Green Again.** An action: one creature at 0 HP within 30 ft that the paladin can see regains HP equal to the paladin's level and stands, and the paladin loses as many HP, which nothing reduces.
- **3, Channel Divinity: What Falls, Feeds.** A reaction, when a creature the paladin can see drops to 0 HP within 30 ft: each friend within 10 ft of the one who fell regains 1d6 + the paladin's CHA modifier.
- **7, Aura of Deep Roots.** The paladin and friends within 10 ft cannot be moved against their will or knocked prone (each may choose to be). At 18, 30 ft.
- **15, 20:** waiting.

**NOT** the Oath of the Ancients, the PHB's green oath, though Kalindel is the nature of the planet: its Nature's Wrath restrains with vines, its Turn the Faithless turns fey and fiends, its aura gives resistance to spell damage -- this one restrains nothing, turns nothing and wards against no spell. Not Vengeance (no vow of advantage, no fear). Not the SRD's Devotion (no blessed weapon, no turning, no charm-proof aura). The nearest outside the PHB is the Crown's Turn the Tide (a bonus action that heals everyone hurt nearby); What Falls, Feeds heals only those beside a foe that has just fallen. **The engines:** being pushed and knocked prone are on the grid (shove, the Sand's knockdown, Repelling Blast, Thunderwave, the roper's Reel).

### 2.4 Ours, built, read against the PHB (CLOSE)

- **Cutthroat** (the rogue at 3, Vivian's other road): *Opening Cut -- in the first round, advantage on a foe that hasn't acted yet, and a hit on it is a critical* sits on the PHB Assassin's Assassinate (advantage on a creature that hasn't taken a turn, and a critical on a surprised one). The trigger and both effects are the Assassin's. A redraft, offered: **Opening Cut** -- the first time in a fight the rogue's Sneak Attack lands, the foe's speed is 0 till the end of its next turn (a hamstring). It gives no advantage and no critical. The name stays.
- **The Path of the Sand's Answer Back** (the barbarian at 6): *one who misses in melee gets a blow back* has the trigger and effect of the PHB Battle Master's Riposte (a maneuver: a reaction attack on a melee miss), without the die and on another class. Lighter than Cutthroat; his look.
- **Read clean:** the Window, the Vigil, the Keeping, the Grey Road (Turn It Aside's nearest is the SRD's Protection style, with another effect), the Mirror, the Rimeglass, the Sand's First Blood and Down in the Sand.
