# The level choices, for DRAGONSLEEP

## 0. His rulings

- **The count (RULED 10-29):** *"I want the players to be making the same decisions at level up as they would be playing in a version using the PHB and DMG, and where we have to we will event the options they're choosing between"* -- each decision is counted as PHB-and-DMG play counts it, and where the SRD has fewer, ours are invented to make up the count.
- **The test of "distinct" (10-30):** *"my metric is whether it can be built within the claude constitution"* -- what must be ours is the expression: our names, our words, our own whole of features, and no PHB text. A game mechanic on its own (a reaction blow on a miss, two weapons swung) may sit near a PHB one. The NOT lines below name the nearest PHB option so he can see the space; they are not a bar a draft must clear.
- **The names (REVOKED 10-30):** the SD initials of 10-29 (*"when applicable, our sufficiently distinct will be acronyms SD"*) are revoked: *"the amount in questions renders my acronym game silly to the point of absurdity. Revoke. (stab debut is fine tho)"*; *"good names instead of silly rules"*. Stab Debut keeps its name.
- **Feats open by score (RULED 10-30, BUILT):** *"approaching SRD compliance silences this issue"* -- as the PHB's, every feat is any class's that meets its prerequisite; Symmetric Fighting is the barbarian's and the paladin's at STR 17 as much as the fighter's.
- **The whole lists (10-30):** *"SRD we haven't built yet may address my chief concerns"* -- no inventions where the SRD's list is the PHB's; there, the work is building the SRD's own.
- **The choice at 3 (10-30):** *"SRD compliance failures in this area have been a large oversight and are of increased priority re: usage"*; then *"talk over what's to be built"* -- talked over in the seat's report, built on his word.
- **Multiclassing (RULED 10-30):** *"its exclude from storyworld game for now, will need for pocket DM after deep16 wrap"*.
- **Stab Debut's crits (RULED 10-30, BUILT):** *"restore the 'on crit' properties to it as well, shallow rebench"*.

Each decision gets one verdict:

- **SHORT n**: PHB-and-DMG play offers more than the SRD; n of ours are wanted to make up the count. Drafted here for his word.
- **WHOLE**: the SRD's list is the PHB's list (the 2014 rules): nothing to invent; the work is building the SRD's own.
- **OURS**: our own, built and in the game.
- **DRAFT**: our own, drafted here; nothing built till his word.

**The playable levels:** the first game caps at 9 (`R.CAP`); the second game's ladders and the Pocket DM go to 12. The drafts are written to 9; a subclass's 10th-and-later features wait in its row.

## 2. The first batch: what the four meet first

### 2.1 The fighter at 3: the Threshing Floor (BUILT 10-30, the grid)

Barley's road: *"He went down once against Cutter on fight night and got back up"* (`content/heroes.json`), a Dominion farm wanderer with a threshing flail. A fighter who fights from the ground, gets up off it, and beats down what is on it.

- **3, Hard Ground.** Prone is no trouble to the fighter: its melee attacks while prone have no disadvantage, a creature within 5 ft has no advantage against it for its being prone, and standing up costs 5 ft of movement.
- **3, Up Off the Floor.** Once per long rest, when the fighter is dropped to 0 HP and not killed, it rises at the start of its next turn with HP equal to its fighter level + its CON modifier, standing at no cost, and its first attack that turn has advantage.
- **7, Threshing.** Once on each of its turns, when the fighter hits a prone creature with a melee weapon attack, the hit deals one more weapon die.
- **10, 15, 18:** waiting, past the cap.

**NOT** the Battle Master (no superiority dice, no maneuvers -- it knocks nothing down, it fights what is down), nor the Eldritch Knight (no spells). Not the SRD's own Relentless Endurance (the half-orc's) or Relentless Rage (the barbarian's): those keep a creature from falling, and this one falls, loses its time, then gets back up. **The engines:** prone is on the grid (its prone rows, the bulette's knockdown, the Sand's, Grappler's PIN); a hero at 0 is down (KO) with no death saves in either battle, so the rise is at the start of its next turn. **Built 10-30** on his *"i'd say yes"*: `js/rules.js` R.SUBCLASSES.fighter (the Pocket DM's maker asks it at 3) and R.champion (the Champion's Improved Critical and Remarkable Athlete, every fighter's but ours); `deep16/js/rules.js` RU.hardGround, RU.riseCost, RU.startTurn (the rise, and up for 5 ft at the turn's start -- the Grey Road's Pit-Wise too, which had paid half there), RU.edges; `deep16/js/battle.js` (the rise's advantage spent, Threshing); `deep16/js/classes.js` (the use a long rest); the FEATURES shelf (`deep16/data/features.js`, `deep16/js/gallery.js`); `mode=threshing1030`. Not in the first game: Barley's sheet keeps the Champion.

### 2.2 The paladin at 3: the Oath of the Green Return (DRAFT)

Lymen's road: *"Self-ordained under Kalindel, the generative nature of the planet, in a town outside Newland's writ"* (`content/heroes.json`). An oath that gives back what the field takes.

- **Tenets.** What falls, feeds. Leave the ground richer than you found it. No salted earth.
- **Oath spells** (all SRD, none on Devotion's or the Ancients' lists): 3rd, Goodberry and Longstrider; 5th, Barkskin and Spike Growth; 9th, Daylight and Meld into Stone. Built on the grid: Longstrider, Barkskin, Spike Growth, Daylight; not built: Goodberry, Meld into Stone (a duration only).
- **3, Channel Divinity: Green Again.** An action: one creature at 0 HP within 30 ft that the paladin can see regains HP equal to the paladin's level and stands, and the paladin loses as many HP, which nothing reduces.
- **3, Channel Divinity: What Falls, Feeds.** A reaction, when a creature the paladin can see drops to 0 HP within 30 ft: each friend within 10 ft of the one who fell regains 1d6 + the paladin's CHA modifier.
- **7, Aura of Deep Roots.** The paladin and friends within 10 ft cannot be moved against their will or knocked prone (each may choose to be). At 18, 30 ft.
- **15, 20:** waiting.

**NOT** the Oath of the Ancients, the PHB's green oath, though Kalindel is the nature of the planet: its Nature's Wrath restrains with vines, its Turn the Faithless turns fey and fiends, its aura gives resistance to spell damage -- this one restrains nothing, turns nothing and wards against no spell. Not Vengeance (no vow of advantage, no fear), not the DMG's Oathbreaker (no undead, no aura of hate). Not the SRD's Devotion (no blessed weapon, no turning, no charm-proof aura). The nearest outside the PHB is the Crown's Turn the Tide (a bonus action that heals everyone hurt nearby); What Falls, Feeds heals only those beside a foe that has just fallen. **The engines:** being pushed and knocked prone are on the grid (shove, the Sand's knockdown, Repelling Blast, Thunderwave, the roper's Reel).

### 2.4 Ours, built, read against the PHB

- **Cutthroat** (the rogue at 3, Vivian's other road): **BUILT, Stab Debut** in place of Opening Cut (10-29, his *"replace 'opening cut' with 'Stab Debut' where she gets a straight weapon damage attack (no bonuses) on targets that haven't first-turned"*): with the Attack action at a foe that has not had its first turn, one more attack at it, the weapon's dice alone -- no ability modifier, no Sneak Attack, no rider, no advantage from it. **A critical's own extras ride it (BUILT 10-30, his *"restore the 'on crit' properties to it as well"*):** a 20 doubles its dice, Savage Attacks and Brutal Critical add theirs, a weapon's on-a-critical (the Winnower's) holds. The seat's reading: free, once a turn, the same foe while it stands. Both battles (`js/battle.js` heroAttack, `deep16/js/battle.js` stabDebut); `mode=stabdebut1029`, `dev/bench8.py stabdebut1029`. **What it cut** (the expected first blow against AC 13-15, Sneak Attack landing both ways): about 18 to 9.5 at 3rd level with the candle-knife, 27 to 14.5 at 5th, 35.5 to 19 at 8th -- most of it the old critical's doubled Sneak Attack.
- **The Path of the Sand's Answer Back** (the barbarian at 6): a reaction blow on a melee miss, the mechanic of the PHB Battle Master's Riposte, in our own words, name and path. By his metric (§0) it stands.
- **Read clean:** the Window, the Vigil, the Keeping, the Grey Road, the Mirror, the Rimeglass, the Sand's First Blood and Down in the Sand.
