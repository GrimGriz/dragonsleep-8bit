Seed 31679 (level 3: won in round 6, one flood, one wall; the flood needs the wall now, so floods are rare) replays identically drained and through the page's frame loop; dev/keeper-probe.py checks it. - **PLAY THE KEEPER** (a human is the Keeper, the class AI the party): `?keeperfight&play=keeper&lvl=3` (add `&lit` to see the stair; the entry card wants E). Its turn is the game's own ring (Q, or E/X on the Keeper; 1-9; MOVE then a gold square) with only what applies on it: MOVE (then a gold square to pick), SLAM <hero> (DC 15 STR or prone), THE WAVE (bonus), READY THE ICE WALL, SWIRL <hero> (one on the deep), ACTIVE SUFFOCATION (bonus, swirling), LET GO, BREAK FREE (a bonus action, then the action, when frozen), END TURN. Its reaction (the opportunity attack) is automatic.
- **PLAY OR SCRIPT THE PARTY** (the Keeper is the AI): `?keeperfight&play=party&lvl=3` -- the party is played by the keys and mouse as ever, or by `D16.keeperPlay` from the console, one call a turn:
  - `D16.keeperPlay.state()`: JSON, the whole state: `pending` (a hero's `turn`, a `prompt` with its options, `running`, `over`), every unit (id, square and its `lane` frame, HP, AC, conditions, `held` by whom and how, `prone`, `hidden`, the turn's move/action/bonus), the Keeper (`swirling` whom, `frozen`, `hidden`), the wall's sections and HP, the ice squares, the map's `geo` and `deeps`, and for the hero deciding `legal`: reachable squares, who it can strike, the wall's squares it can strike, the swirl's squares, the spells it can cast, the other actions.
  - `await D16.keeperPlay.act({ move: {x,y} | {a,c}, do: 'attack'|'cast'|'dodge'|'dash'|'disengage'|'help'|'breakfree'|'hide'|'none', target: '<id>' | {x,y} | {wall:[x,y]} | {units:[ids]}, spell: '<id>', slot: n, keep: true })`: the move, then the action, then the turn ends (`keep: true` leaves the turn his); a Promise of the state at the next decision, the page's frame loop running the Keeper's and the others' turns between. A prompt the game puts to the hero (an opportunity attack, a dash) comes back as `pending.type === 'prompt'` and is answered with `act({ answer: <value> })`.
  - `D16.keeperPlay.actSync(plan)`: the same, stepping the game itself (the probe, a page with no frame loop): returns the state.
  The units' ids are the fixture's (`barley`, `aurdin`, `vivian`, `lymen`) and `keeper`. Driven from a page with the frame loop running (a timer stepping `D16.update`), a script played 14 turns of a fight through `act`, one prompt answered; the probe plays a whole fight through `actSync`.
- **Pose swap in the pane** (Griz: neither sheet is canon): in the console, `D16.keeper.pose({ slam: 'slam_ba' })` (the Slam's dive the other way about) or any row: `pose({ idle: 'b_idle', wave: 'a_wave', wall: 'b_wall', slam: 'b_dive' })`. Rows: `a_*` the first Grok sheet's, `b_*` the second's (idle, wall, dive, wave); `stand` (the default idle: the third sheet's standing poses, a facing each); `slam_b3a` (the default Slam: the second sheet's dive with the first sheet's frame 3), `slam_ab` (the first sheet's dive to the launch, the second's the smash and return), `slam_ba`; `hurt_b`, `die_b`. Defaults in `D16.keeper.POSE`. keeper_p3 holds what is drawn about him: the water swirl, the ice-wall form (frames 4-6, the solid wall, are what stands). Settings in `D16.keeper.CFG`: `hideAfter` (back into the water unseen after its turn), `oaWave` (its opportunity attack a wave toward the deep: not ruled, off), `freezeNeeds` ('all' four squares frozen to hold it, or 'any'), `iceDC` 7, `wallStrikeAC` 10, `wallScale`, `swirlScale`.

## Where rows 6 and 7 are ("in the pool, where nothing can be sealed in")
`floodstair` is 18 wide. Rows 1-7 are the pool (row 7: water x4-12; row 6: x3-13), rows 8-13 the stair. The Ice Wall covers the lane, x7-10, four squares. **Only rows 11, 12 and 13 are exactly four wide** (`#######====#######`), so a wall across x7-10 there closes the stair completely: that is what "seals the party in with the Keeper" needs, and why 11 was the row. Row 10 and row 9 are six wide (x6-11: a floor square either side of the stair), row 8 eight wide (x5-12), the pool at 6 and 7 nine to eleven wide. A wall across x7-10 on any of those has an open square on each side, so a hero walks round it (at 7: (6,7), (5,7)... or the shore at (6,8)); nothing is sealed in or out. Also, the party enters on rows 8-10 and the Keeper sits at (8,4): a wall on 6 or 7 is between them and it, not behind them. In the code, `canReadyWall` asks for a hero on the pool side of the row, and the party starts south of 6 and 7, so the Keeper never readies it: hence 0 springs, and the same table. Wall `rowOverride` is a plain number, so nothing is lost by it; a "full-width" wall at 6/7 (every square of the row) would be a different feature, not built.

## The combat log (js/keeperlog.js, 10-03)

- Written by all three: the watched fight, `play=keeper` (the ring commands appear as `kslam`, `kwave`, `kcast`, ...) and `play=party` (the script's commands). An opportunity attack inside a hero's move is part of that move's line (its rolls are there); `hpAfter` is every combatant's HP after the action; the flags are the state after it.

The AI Keeper's fights are unchanged (30 fights at level 3: same numbers).
- Other `u.weapon` reads checked (ui.js 217/277/609/1480/1481/1839/1892/1915, battle.js 646/757-761/799/879/937/957/1453/1551/1558/1728/1730/1954, ai.js 872/901): the ones a weapon-less foe could reach are covered by the stand-in; the rest are guarded or are the heroes'.
