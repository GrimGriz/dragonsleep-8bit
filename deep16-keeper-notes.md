  - `await D16.keeperPlay.act({ move: {x,y} | {a,c}, do: 'attack'|'cast'|'dodge'|'dash'|'disengage'|'help'|'breakfree'|'hide'|'none', target: '<id>' | {x,y} | {wall:[x,y]} | {units:[ids]}, spell: '<id>', slot: n, keep: true })`: the move, then the action, then the turn ends (`keep: true` leaves the turn his); a Promise of the state at the next decision, the page's frame loop running the Keeper's and the others' turns between. A prompt the game puts to the hero (an opportunity attack, a dash) comes back as `pending.type === 'prompt'` and is answered with `act({ answer: <value> })`.
  - `D16.keeperPlay.actSync(plan)`: the same, stepping the game itself (the probe, a page with no frame loop): returns the state.
  The units' ids are the fixture's (`barley`, `aurdin`, `vivian`, `lymen`) and `keeper`. Driven from a page with the frame loop running (a timer stepping `D16.update`), a script played 14 turns of a fight through `act`, one prompt answered; the probe plays a whole fight through `actSync`.
