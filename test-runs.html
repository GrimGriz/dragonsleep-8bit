    The class floor?npc= · vs= · lvl= · watch

    The foes (npc=)

    A class (druid:3:dwarf: its own level, a race), a named character (talmok:7; the grown build talmok::grown), or any monster of the bestiary by name (hyena). The count writes the word that many times.

    Level (lvl=)

    lvl

    1 to 12; the game's default is 5. Only the druid and Pyro go past 9, and our four stop at 9. It sets every row that carries no level of its own (a named character with a register level, Talmok's 3 or Torvald's 5, keeps it unless the row says another).

    Your side (vs=)

    Empty: you run our four. Add rows to run a band of your choosing instead. (The game reads classes and named characters here, not bestiary monsters.)

    spell=

    only (only=: keeps to these; they play in the order you pick them)

    foe=

    Keys on the stage: left/right the next spell, up/down jump ten, E casts again, M the menu. foe= puts three of that bestiary monster where the three fighters stand.

    The feature gallery?fxgallery&features

    feature=

    only (only=: keeps to these; they play in the order you pick them)

    The feature gallery has no keep or foe=: those are the spell gallery's.
